In this experiment, you will determine whether AI-generated texts contain watermarks.
Watermarks are hidden information embedded in the text to enable future identification of AI-generated content.

During the experiment:
    * You will complete 4 short tasks using the AI we provide
    * In each task, you will identify whether the AI response contains a watermark
    * If you think a watermark exists, you will explain why and try to remove it without changing the meaning

You may use any external tools or materials for translation or watermark identification.

To enable copy/paste from the application to external tools, make sure your keyboard language is set to English.

If you encounter a problem, you can re-enter and continue from the same point.
